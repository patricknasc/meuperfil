# MeuPerfil — Pitch

## Positioning

MeuPerfil is a content-to-conversion platform.

It is not a generic social media scheduler, not a generic chatbot platform, and not a simple link-in-bio tool.

The product exists to help creators, businesses, and teams turn attention into measurable action.

## Core promise

Turn every post into a campaign.

## Value proposition

When a user publishes content, the system should help them transform it into a clear conversion flow:

Content -> Engagement -> Action -> Conversion

This includes:

- publishing content
- creating a CTA
- triggering conversations
- sending private replies or DMs
- connecting tracked links
- capturing leads
- measuring campaign performance

## Why this matters

Most businesses and creators already publish content, but they struggle with the gap between attention and conversion.

The problem is not posting more content. The real problem is that content is disconnected from:

- CTAs
- automation
- conversion flows
- measurable results

MeuPerfil closes that gap.

## The product story

MeuPerfil helps people stop treating content as isolated posts and start treating it as a campaign asset.

A campaign can contain:

- one or more content pieces
- a specific offer or CTA
- a trigger such as a keyword comment
- a DM or private response
- a tracked link or landing page
- conversion metrics and attribution

MeuPerfil helps creators, brands, and businesses turn content into campaigns that generate conversations, leads, and conversions without stitching together multiple disconnected tools.

## The category

Content-to-Conversion Platform

Alternative names:

- Campaign OS
- Conversion platform for creators and businesses
- Social growth and conversion engine

## Target customers

Primary:

- creators
- experts
- consultants
- infoprodutores
- small businesses with content and an offer

Secondary:

- agencies managing multiple campaigns
- brands with regular content and conversion goals
- teams running offers, launch funnels, and lead generation campaigns

## Differentiators

- campaign-first thinking instead of feature-first thinking
- content-linked conversion rather than generic content scheduling
- conversion tracking connected to the actual campaign
- better fit for content-led growth than standard social management tools
- works as a platform that can expand into multiple verticals later

## Strategic principle

Do not build a generic social media manager.

Build a system where one piece of content can become a measurable conversion campaign.

## Main product message

The product should not be sold as a collection of features.

It should be sold as a system that turns content into action.

## Final pitch statement

MeuPerfil is the platform that helps creators and businesses turn content into campaigns, conversations, leads, and measurable conversion.

It is the bridge between content and growth.
